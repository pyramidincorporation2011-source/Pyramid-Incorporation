Use these technically accurate descriptions in `src/data/services.js`. Each service needs:

The services:

1. **Toilet / Bathroom Waterproofing.** Most bathroom leaks pass through the floor, wall–floor junctions and pipe penetrations into the room below or the adjoining walls. We prepare the surface, seal pipe outlets and penetrations, form coving at junctions, apply a flexible polymer-modified cementitious or liquid membrane across the floor and up the walls, and pond-test before tiling.
2. **Sump / Underground Water Tank Waterproofing.** Underground tanks face water pressure from inside and groundwater from outside. We treat construction joints and pipe inlets, repair honeycombed concrete, and apply cementitious or crystalline waterproofing. For drinking-water tanks we use materials suitable for potable water storage.
3. **Terrace Insulation & Treatment.** We repair cracks, correct slopes and drainage outlets, and apply a waterproofing system suited to the terrace (elastomeric coatings or membranes), with heat-reflective coatings or insulation layers to bring down slab temperature and the heat entering the rooms below.
4. **Expansion Joint Treatment.** Expansion joints let a structure move with temperature and load; failed joints let water straight in. We clean out old fillers, insert backer rod and seal with elastomeric sealants (such as PU or polysulphide), or fit joint-cover systems, so the joint stays watertight while still allowing movement.
5. **Crack Treatment.** We first assess whether a crack is surface-level or deeper, and dormant or still moving. Then we treat it accordingly: V-grooving and filling with polymer-modified mortar or flexible sealant, or injection for deeper cracks. Cracks that indicate structural distress are referred for a structural engineer's assessment.
6. **Cement Slurry Grouting.** Cement-based grout is pumped under pressure through injection nozzles to fill voids, honeycombing and porous concrete. This consolidates the member and closes the paths water uses to seep through.
7. **PU Grouting.** Polyurethane resin is injected through packers into actively leaking cracks and joints. It reacts with water and expands to seal the leak, even under wet conditions, which makes it suited to basements, tanks, lift pits and other below-ground areas.
8. **Core Cutting.** Diamond core drilling makes clean, precise circular openings in concrete and masonry for pipes, drains and service lines. It causes far less vibration and surrounding damage than breaking with hammers.
9. **Rebaring.** Corroded reinforcement is exposed, cleaned, treated with anti-corrosion coating and re-covered with polymer-modified repair mortar. Where reinforcement must be added, new bars are anchored into drilled holes with chemical anchoring. Waterproofing is then applied to stop the corrosion from returning.
10. **Basement Treatment.** Below-ground walls and floors are under constant water pressure. We combine injection grouting for active leaks, joint sealing, and cementitious or crystalline coatings to control seepage, dampness and the damage they cause.

## 4. Design direction

The site should feel premium, engineered and trustworthy, like an established technical contractor, not a template.

- **Palette:**
  - Deep navy `#0B1F3A` (primary)
  - Ink navy `#071427` (darkest sections)
  - Off-white `#F6F4EF` (light sections)
  - Charcoal `#2A2E35` (body text)
  - Warm grey `#8A8F98` (secondary text)
  - Copper-gold accent `#C8862E` (used sparingly: CTAs, rules, numerals)
  - No rainbow gradients; at most subtle navy-to-ink overlays on photos.
- **Type:**
  - Self-host fonts with `@fontsource` (no Google Fonts CDN).
  - Headings: a strong, slightly condensed grotesk such as **Archivo** (600–800).
  - Body: **Inter**.
  - Use a clear scale, generous line-height and tight letter-spacing on large headings.
  - Small uppercase tracked eyebrow labels with section numbers ("01 — About").
- **Shape language:**
  - Mostly sharp corners (2–4px radius maximum); no bubbly cards.
  - Thin hairline borders and an architectural grid feel.
  - The pyramid triangle as a recurring motif: logo mark, bullet markers, section dividers, subtle background linework.
- **Layout:**
  - Asymmetric compositions; full-bleed photography; alternate light and dark sections.
  - Don't repeat the same "three equal cards" layout in every section.
- **Motion:**
  - Use `motion` (Framer Motion) for restrained reveal-on-scroll (fade/slide 16–24px), a hero headline stagger, and image hover zoom on cards.
  - Respect `prefers-reduced-motion`.
  - No parallax overload, no bouncing.
- **Logo:**
  - Design a new SVG logo: a geometric pyramid mark (two-tone navy and copper, with a subtle water-drop or layer cue) plus a "PYRAMID INCORPORATION" wordmark.
  - Produce light and dark versions.
  - Use the mark for the favicon set: `favicon.svg`, `favicon.ico`, `apple-touch-icon.png`, 192/512 PNGs and `site.webmanifest`.

  - `src/hooks/`.

  - Title: "Pyramid Incorporation | Waterproofing Contractors in Nagpur since 2010"

  - Open Graph and Twitter tags, plus a 1200×630 OG image you generate

- **Accessibility:**
  - WCAG AA contrast, visible focus states, skip-to-content link

- **Deployment config:**
